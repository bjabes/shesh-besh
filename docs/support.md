# Gammonade Support

## What it is

Gammonade is a backgammon game for iPhone. Three ways to play:

- **Practice vs AI** — three difficulty levels (Easy, Medium, Hard).
- **Pass-and-play** — local match against a friend on the same device.
- **Game Center** — turn-based asynchronous matches with anyone signed into Game Center.

The app tracks ongoing rivalries with full match history.

## Requirements

- iPhone running iOS 17 or later
- Game Center sign-in (only required for online multiplayer)

## How to start a match

1. Open the app — you'll land on the rivalries screen.
2. Tap **+ AI Rival** to play offline, or **+ Game Center Rival** to invite someone.
3. Pick a match length (number of points to win).
4. Take turns rolling and moving until one player wins.

## Reporting a bug

Two options:

- **Email:** _to be filled in by the developer before launch_
- **GitHub Issues:** [bjabes/shesh-besh/issues](https://github.com/bjabes/shesh-besh/issues) — include device model, iOS version, and steps to reproduce.

If a Game Center match gets stuck, taking your turn from any signed-in device usually unblocks it.

## Privacy

See the [Privacy policy](../privacy/). Short version: we collect nothing; Game Center handles online match data.

## Known limitations

- iPhone only — no iPad layout yet.
- Portrait orientation only.
- No achievements or leaderboards.
- No iCloud sync; rivals and history live on the device they were created on.
