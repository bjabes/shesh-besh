# {{ site.title }} Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-04-28_

{{ site.title }} is built to respect your privacy. The summary: we don't collect anything ourselves. The only data that leaves your device is what Game Center handles when you choose to play multiplayer.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** {{ site.title }} does not run analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or third-party tracking SDKs. The app does not contact any server we operate.

## What Game Center handles

When you play a turn-based match against another person, Apple's Game Center service (not us) processes:

- Your Game Center display name and player ID
- The state of the match (board position, dice rolls, turn metadata) so the next player can see it on their device
- Turn-completion notifications

This data is governed by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) and the Game Center settings on your device. Sign-in is optional — the AI mode and pass-and-play do not require Game Center.

## What stays on your device

The app keeps a local file (`ledger.json` inside the app's sandbox) with:

- Rivals you create (display names, optional Game Center associations)
- Completed match outcomes (scores, winner)
- In-progress matches you can resume

This file never leaves your device. It is removed when you uninstall the app.

## Children

{{ site.title }} is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

## Tracking

We do not track you. The app declares no `User Tracking` purpose, requests no IDFA, and uses no third-party tracking SDKs.

## In-app purchases and ads

There are none.
